Mains is now [open source under the MIT license](https://github.com/mainsdotdev/mains). This release also makes the work easier to see and get back to: answers can be interactive, search reaches across your runs, and a Mac window can become context for your next prompt.

### answers you can interact with

- **Codex can show, not just tell:** Ask for a chart, sortable table, or simulation and it can appear right in the transcript. Your choices in it are remembered when you return, and a control can suggest a follow-up prompt.
- **MCP Apps appear beside the work:** When a connected tool provides its own interface, Mains shows it inside that tool's row. You can sign in to supported connectors from Mains.

Both features are available with Codex in this release.

### find and bring in context

- **Search all your work:** Press `⌘⌥K` to find commands, workspaces, runs, and documents. A run can also be found by something said in its conversation.
- **Capture any window:** Turn on Lens, then press `⌘⇧Space` over another Mac app. Its frontmost window is attached to your next message, with readable text from that window when Accessibility is enabled.
- **Browse without losing your place:** The in-app browser now has tabs, history, downloads, and a resizable device view for checking a page at different sizes.

Lens adds context to the composer; it does not send a prompt on its own.

### stay close to the work

When an agent needs an answer or finishes a run, Mains can notify you. Simple approvals and questions can be answered from the notification or menu bar. After a turn, a change card shows the files it edited, a diff, and an **Undo** action when the changes can still be safely reversed.

The session panel also brings sources and deliverables together, and the document viewer can now open PDFs.
