0.14 gives the pages your agents work on more room, and lets a chat reach beyond its workspace when a task spans more than one folder.

### browse with the chat on top

Click **Expand browser** and the browser takes over the whole window. Your chat comes along as a floating card over the live page, so you can keep talking to the agent while you scroll, click, and check its work.

- **Pick a size:** Show the full conversation, shrink it to just the composer, or minimize it to an icon. Clicks outside the card go straight to the page.
- **See progress at a glance:** While a run is going, the compact chat shows how long it has been working and what it's doing right now.


It's the same chat you had before expanding, with your draft, model, and attachments. **Restore browser panel** brings the usual layout back.

### give a chat another folder

Claude and Codex chats can now work with folders outside their workspace. Type `/add-dir` to choose a folder, or follow it with a path to add one directly. Added folders show up in the composer, where you can remove access at any time, and a chat keeps its folders when you continue it later.

### see what agents made

- **Image galleries:** When a turn generates several images, they share one large preview with a row of thumbnails to switch between.

### smaller changes

- **Plugins in every space:** The navigation rail now shows Plugins in Work and Chat too, not just Code.
- **Tidier session panel:** Sources fold away until you open them.
- **Line counts on edits:** File edits and new files show how many lines were added and removed, right on the row.
