An agent does not always finish before you step away from your desk. A task might still be running, or you might remember a detail worth adding after opening another device.

## A different screen for the same work

Remote control lets another device connect to the Mains backend. The agent, workspace files, and terminals stay on the backend machine while the interface follows you.

From a phone or another computer, check the conversation and send the next instruction. The work stays in the workspace where it began.

## Returning to the desk

When you return, open the same run to see the conversation and review its changes. There is no separate copy of the project to reconcile before continuing.
