A completed run is the beginning of a review. The agent has made a change; now you need to understand what it did and whether the result fits the project.

## Reading the change as a whole

Open the review view to move through the files in one place. Expand the diffs you need, follow the surrounding code, and leave a comment where a detail needs attention.

The comment goes back to the agent with its file and line, giving the next turn a concrete place to start.

## Keeping feedback close

When the conversation sits beside the diff, it is easier to ask for one focused adjustment and then inspect the result. Review becomes part of the work instead of another task to reconstruct later.
