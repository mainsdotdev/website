A small idea is easier to try when it has a place of its own. Maybe you want to compare two implementations, explore a new interaction, or let an agent work on a bug while you review something else.

## Starting with a separate working tree

Mains gives each workspace its own Git working tree. An agent can change files on its branch while another workspace stays focused on a different task.

That separation makes the next action clear. Open the workspace to check the run, look through its changes, or send a follow-up with the context it needs.

## Reviewing one task at a time

Before bringing a change back, read the diff as one piece of work. Leave feedback where a detail needs attention and let the agent make another pass.

An experiment can stay an experiment until it earns a place in the project. The useful parts are ready to commit; the next idea can start in another workspace.
